## Why Choose Eyelash Extensions?
 Eyelash extensions are designed to add length, volume, and curl to your natural lashes. Unlike strip lashes, they are individually applied for a natural and long-lasting finish. With proper care, eyelash extensions can last several weeks, making them a convenient beauty solution for busy lifestyles.

## How to Care for Your Lash Extensions
 To keep your extensions looking their best, avoid rubbing your eyes, use oil-free makeup removers, and gently brush your lashes daily. Following the recommended aftercare routine helps maintain their beauty and extends their lifespan.
